Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often about how Healthier Soles works, our impact, and how to get involved.

01

How much of the money is actually donated?

All proceeds go directly toward shoe distributions for communities in need.

02

Will you expand?

Yes—we're always open to new partnerships that help us reach more people.

03

What is the impact?

We help prevent soil-transmitted diseases, support access to education, and improve long-term health through reliable footwear.

04

How can I join your team?

You can get involved by volunteering, partnering with us, or supporting our mission through donations or sponsorships.

05

How can we reach you?

You can contact us anytime through our website (healthiersoles.org), email (info@healthiersoles.org), or social channels (Instagram: healthier.soles).
