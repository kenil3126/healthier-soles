Why we're raising funds

Every day, kids in underserved communities walk barefoot or in worn-out shoes, exposing them to soil-borne diseases and injuries that are entirely preventable. Healthier Soles exists to change that, one pair of shoes at a time.

We raised $30,000 for our 2025 distribution trip to Ghana, providing shoes specifically designed for the region's terrain. Now we're building toward our next trip on December 10th, 2026 — and we need your help to get there.
